A 100-word monologue spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. The tiredness lives in the voice, the rhythm and the small deflections — never in a plain statement that he is weary — and he is still a villain, with a scheme and a history. The Eternal Flame needs its oil, the hero keeps breaking in and stealing his favorite teacup, and the prophecy says a child with a wooden sword will overthrow him. Honestly, he would welcome it — at least then he could stop worrying about whether the lava moat's insurance is up to date.

Run it with `node main.ts`.
